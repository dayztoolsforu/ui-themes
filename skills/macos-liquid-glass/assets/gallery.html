Your system is set to reduce transparency, so the glass shows as solid panels. That is the kit's accessibility fallback. Turn on Show glass to preview the real material.

Typography

The eleven macOS text styles from Apple's typography guidelines. Sizes are fixed on the Mac; 13 px Body is the default and 10 px is the smallest. Spacing follows the SF Pro tracking table. SF Pro only ships on Apple devices, so other systems show the closest system font.

Materials

Regular glass for anything with text. Clear glass only over photos or video, with a 35% dimming layer when the image is bright. Content-layer materials are for cards and backgrounds, not controls.

Refraction and reflection

The curved edge bends what is behind it, like a thick lens, while the middle stays flat and readable. A highlight runs along the rim on the side facing the light, with a fainter reflection on the far edge. Drag the lens around and change the settings.

Refraction needs a Chromium browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave, Arc). This browser shows the frost and highlights only.

Grow and shrink

Liquid Glass warps as it changes. The edge in front moves first and the edge behind catches up, so glass stretches, overshoots and wobbles into place. Shapes closer than the container spacing melt together through a liquid neck that thins and snaps as they separate. Drag a droplet away to pull on it.

Scrolling under glass

Content runs underneath the toolbar and the floating bar at the bottom. As it scrolls past, the glass frosts it, bends it at the edges and catches light from it. Scroll the window, or leave Auto-scroll on and watch the edges of each bar.
